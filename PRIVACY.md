# Privacy Policy — Touch Enabled Mac

_Last updated: July 17, 2026_

This policy covers the **TE Mac — Touch Enabled Mac** Chrome extension and
the **Touch Enabled Mac** macOS app.

## Summary

Touch Enabled Mac does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.
There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

## What the extension processes

- **Text you type on the on-screen keyboard.** To show word predictions, the
  word you are currently typing is sent to the Datamuse API
  (`api.datamuse.com`). When you type in the YouTube search box, your query
  is sent to YouTube's own suggestion service
  (`suggestqueries-clients6.youtube.com`) to show live search suggestions —
  the same requests the YouTube page itself makes. These requests contain
  only the typed text; no identifiers, cookies, or browsing history are
  attached by the extension. Responses are used immediately and are not
  stored.
- **Your on/off preference.** A single on/off setting is stored locally in
  your browser (`chrome.storage.local`) so the keyboard remembers whether
  you enabled or disabled it. It never leaves your device.

## What the extension does NOT do

- No collection of browsing history, page content, passwords, or form data
- No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
- No cookies and no fingerprinting
- No selling or sharing of any data with third parties
- No remote code — all code ships inside the extension package

## The macOS app

The native macOS app runs entirely on your device and makes no network
requests at all.
